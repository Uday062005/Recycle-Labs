THE COMPANY

The problem

Mixed scrap sells cheaply. Before buying equipment, a dealer cannot tell which crusher, screen and tank settings give a clean, uniform flake, or what purity, recovery, cost and payback to expect.

Our solution

THE PROCESS

From mixed scrap to usable flakes.

Process flowsheet (as simulated in Aspen Plus)

Block names are the Aspen block and stream names. The oversize recycle returns to the mixer.

  • MIX1 adds the fresh feed to the recycled oversize.
  • CRUSH reduces the size; the crusher gap sets the product size, the recycle load and the power.
  • SCREEN1 returns flakes that are still too big (OVERSIZE) to the crusher, so the crusher handles more than the fresh feed.
  • SCREEN2 removes fines as waste.
  • DENSEP is the sink-float tank: PET (1380 kg/m³) sinks to DENSE, PE and PP (950 and 905) float to LIGHT. In Aspen it is a fixed-split separator whose fractions come from the settling-velocity calculation.

Valid operating range

The calculator refuses anything outside these ranges. Results are only validated inside them. No live Aspen run happens when you press the button.

ENGINEERING TRANSPARENCY

Built on physics.
Limited by evidence.

01 / ASPEN PLUS

Mass balance first

Aspen simulates the recycle loop. A case is accepted only if the run history shows the loop converged without errors, fresh feed equals fines + dense + light within 0.1 %, and all size distributions close.

Feed = Fines + Dense + Light
02 / SINK-FLOAT TANK

Settling physics

Flake settling velocity (non-spherical drag, hindered settling) and a smooth partition curve decide what reaches DENSE. The curve's spread and the residence time are assumptions; the sensitivity is shown with every proposal.

P(dense) = Φ((τ/ts − 1)/σ)
03 / MODEL & OPTIMISER

Fast, and checked against Aspen

A Python model of the flowsheet (crusher, screens with recycle, sink-float tank) gives results instantly and matches the Aspen runs to about 1e-4 on every output checked. The optimiser searches the four settings for your goal (payback, profit, recovery or energy) under your size, purity and recovery limits and reports "infeasible" when they cannot be met.

04 / SIZING & COST

Ranges, not single numbers

Motor, screen area and tank volume are sized from the flows; costs are shown as optimistic / base / pessimistic. At 100–500 kg/hr most equipment sits at its practical minimum size, so cost is floor-driven.
